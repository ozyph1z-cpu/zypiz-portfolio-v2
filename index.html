AI engineer + developer · 2025

BeeCatalog

Amazon listings from a product photo. Then from the invoice alone.

I started as a catalog specialist on Amazon listings, entering every product by hand. A batch took about 15 days. In early 2025 I studied AI and built a prototype: upload the image, title and code, and the listing is ready.

V2, which I was building when I left, reads the invoice (NF-e), generates the product images with AI and fills Amazon's own bulk spreadsheet in batches, so it needs no Amazon API.

FAQ

Before you send it

What is AI and what stays real in your ads?
The product stays real: app screens, packs, cards and prices come from real footage. AI makes what you can't shoot, like scenes, voices and characters. Anything a viewer can check on screen matches the footage.
Which platforms do you cut for?
Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and YouTube Shorts, all in vertical 9:16. Each hook is cut as its own variation so you can test them against each other.
What do you need from me?
The offer, any raw footage or screen recordings, and the ads that already work for you. A product page is enough to start.
Can you build the system, not just the video?
Yes. I'm a developer and AI engineer as well as an editor. BeeCatalog and my Claude animation workflow are both systems I designed and built.
How do I send a brief?
Email ozyph1z@gmail.com, message zypizediting on Discord or DM @zyp1z on X. Whichever you use, I answer there.
